Scatterplot Explorer

Direction = slope of the line of best fit

If the line slopes up from left to right → positive correlation. If it slopes down → negative correlation. If it's flat → no correlation.

Strength = distance of points from the line of best fit

Points close to the line → strong correlation (we can predict one score accurately from the other). Points far from the line → weak correlation (predictions are unreliable).

⚠ Common misconception: steep slope = strong correlation

The slope tells you the direction. The distance of points from the line tells you the strength. A steep slope can go with a weak correlation. A gentle slope can go with a strong correlation.
