Construct Validity

Construct validity asks: does this variable really measure the underlying construct of interest? A measure can be perfectly reliable (consistent) but still have poor construct validity — if it is consistently measuring the wrong thing. For each scenario, decide whether the measure has high, questionable, or low construct validity.
