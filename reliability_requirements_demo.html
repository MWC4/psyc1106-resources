Types of Reliability

When do we need test-retest reliability, interrater reliability, both, or neither?
Click any cell to find out.

Test-Retest Reliability

Does the measure give the same score at two different times? Needed when the construct being measured is stable — i.e. not expected to change between measurements. Assessed by correlating scores from Time 1 and Time 2.

Interrater Reliability

Does the measure give the same score regardless of who is doing the rating? Needed when the measurement involves an external observer making a judgment. Assessed by correlating scores from Rater 1 and Rater 2.
